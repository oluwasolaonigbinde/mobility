## Outcome and boundaries

Replace the signed-file agreement across Apply, KYC, renewals, staff review, fixtures, seeds and current contracts with a versioned in-app acceptance. Store the version and server acceptance time on the application, preserve exact retry identity, and show staff the acceptance date/version. Agreement content uses normal plain language without public draft labels; legal approval remains internally outstanding under REQ-032 and existing live-use gates.

Extract the renewal bank list into one shared picker used by Apply and renewal. A backend catalog uses the existing Paystack JSON client when configured and a built-in Nigerian list otherwise; do not duplicate lists in frontend. Popular order: OPay, Moniepoint, PalmPay, Kuda, GTBank, Access, First Bank, UBA, Zenith. Accept actual provider codes including codes longer than three digits.

Apply resolves a selected bank plus exactly ten ASCII account digits through a server adapter, shows the resolved name and requires confirmation. Bind resolution to applicant capability and exact bank/account; never trust a client-supplied resolved name. A provider-unavailable result permits manual name entry and preserves the existing staff bank verification and first-payout D40b gates. No automatic bank verification, transfer or recipient creation. In-flight results become stale on edits; no bank/account/name/capability in browser storage, URLs, logs or ordinary staff lists.

No deployment, force push, live Paystack calls, account-access/tmp reads, legal approval claims, launch queue changes, new money policy or unrelated refactoring. At most two active workers and two test containers; leave mobility demo containers/3100 untouched. Only touched/affected local test files; full suite in GitHub CI.

## Independent plan review disposition

13 October plan review FIX (GPT-6.1-Sol high, codex-fresh-20261012, priority). All four findings accepted; the following concrete rules supersede ambiguous draft wording above.

- Consent is recorded on immutable KYC submissions (version and server timestamp) for every driver, including staff-created profiles without an application. Apply mirrors that consent onto its locked application. Fresh KYC capture requires acceptance; renewal reuses recorded current-version consent, or displays the same agreement/checkbox to supply missing consent. Fresh person/payee approval rejects missing consent. No historical signed upload is treated as acceptance. Tests cover staff-created drivers and missing consent.
- Bank checks return a short-lived signed receipt (15 minutes) bound to driver identity, bank code, account-number digest, outcome and resolved-name digest. No sensitive values are logged or stored outside existing encrypted bank records. Submit requires the receipt plus confirmation for resolved names; typed-name fallback requires an unavailable receipt. Authorization is checked first. Committed exact retries compare the stored receipt fingerprint, bank details and agreement version/acceptance against immutable evidence before current provider/catalog/version/receipt-expiry checks. Locks and transaction rollback cover concurrent/failing submissions; later renewal carries consent provenance without inventing a new acceptance time.
- Unconfigured provider, transport timeout/network error, 5xx or provider 429 permit fallback. Invalid input, 4xx rejection, malformed response or mismatched returned account are errors and do not permit manual capture. Capability denial, local throttling and limiter storage failure never produce fallback receipts. Fake transport and API tests cover these separately.
- Initial agreement version is `1`, with immutable copy in frontend/src/lib/driver-agreement.ts and a full-text /apply/agreement page. The five key points: “You work as an independent contractor.” “Your job offer shows how much you can earn. Pay depends on the driving we verify.” “GPS is tracked only while you are on a trip.” “Keep the advert on your car for the agreed job.” “You agree to our use of your details to check your application, run trips and pay you.” Full text expands only those approved topics: contractor relationship, frozen offer/pay and verified driving, trip-only location capture, advert care/reporting, purpose-limited ID/bank/contact/location processing and contacting Terrax about privacy. Wording is internally a draft awaiting REQ-032; no public draft labels or invented legal/retention values. New versions are separate immutable artifacts; stale fresh submissions fail, committed historical retries keep their recorded version/time.

Verification additions: missing/false/stale consent, staff-created consent, concurrent first acceptance and rollback; forged/expired/wrong-driver/wrong-account/name receipts, retry during provider outage/catalog change, invalid-account no-fallback, local limiter denial/unavailability and provider mismatch.
