# High-risk candidate verification

## Scope and method

Verified all 53 candidates with prefixes `AUT`, `DB`, `MON`, `OFF`, `ONB`,
`PRV`, and `SEC` from `issues/planning/consolidated-findings.md` against
current `master` at `38094d605830ccce111bcb0773ec1a249fed2d58`.

The first-pass audits inspected `637841d95493bcc24334356da42097fa53a5d16f`.
That revision is an ancestor of the current source by two commits. A scoped
source diff found no functional change in the cited authentication, database,
money, onboarding, privacy, or trip-finality authorities. The sole relevant
frontend diff is the Cardvert rename of `DEFAULT_DB_NAME` in
`frontend/src/lib/trips/ping-queue.ts:81-82`; queue/dead-letter behavior is
unchanged. Accordingly, no candidate became stale through source drift.

The recovered complete audit tails added `AUT-006`, `AUT-007`, `DB-007`,
`OFF-009`, and `PRV-009`; that is candidate-provenance recovery, not a source
change. Rechecking their authorities did not alter any previously recorded
current-source classification.

The later settlement normalization added `MON-007`–`MON-009` by splitting
independently testable consequences from `MON-003`, `MON-004`, and `MON-001`.
Changed backlinks on `DB-007`, `MON-001`, `MON-002`, `MON-005`, and `MON-006`
only add or refine provenance; their current-source classifications are unchanged.

After the first Pro review identified a packaging omission, `SEC-002` was
rechecked against the exact repository `Caddyfile`. Its classification and R14
assignment are unchanged. The corrected source bundle now contains that file.

This was a static, read-only verification. No database, provider, browser, or
device test was run. Race findings are marked as unexecuted hypotheses even
where the current lock/order structure confirms the unsafe interleaving.
`EXTERNAL_UNVERIFIABLE` is used only where the claim needs production-like
infrastructure evidence that is absent from the repository, not as a substitute
for a source verdict.
