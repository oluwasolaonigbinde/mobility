# End-to-end and human-facing audit (REQ-180)

Audit of local master `21567e32` on branch `audit/e2e-ux-2026-10-16`, walked 16–18 October 2026.
This report finds and explains problems only. Nothing in the product was changed.

## How this was checked

- **Stack:** one audit container (`cv-audit-e2e`) ran the API, worker, Redis, MinIO, a local mail sink and the
  **production** Next.js build (`node .next/standalone/server.js`) on `http://localhost:3210`. The
  database was a separate `cardvert_audit` database inside the existing `cv-req139-db` Postgres, seeded with
  `python -m app.seeds.demo`. The scanner was the existing `cv-req139-scanner`.
- **Providers:** none. Paystack, Sentry and map-style settings were empty, and email went to the local sink.
  Payments were not exercised through any provider.
- **Browser:** every role was walked in headless Chromium (Playwright 1.61.1) at desktop (1366×800), tablet
  (820×1180) and phone (390×844) sizes.
  - Screenshots and page-text dumps are under `.tmp-audit/shots/{adv,drv,adm,pub,flows}/`. They are not committed.
  - A screenshot path below such as `flows/x.png` means `.tmp-audit/shots/flows/x.png`.
- **Hand-offs walked live:**
  - Public enquiry.
  - Driver application, start to finish: apply → code email → ID and bank → staff review → car → staff car
    approval → account set-up email → driver sign-in → staff job offer → driver accepts → installation
    photo screen.
  - Forgot/reset password.
  - Notifications, including "Mark all read".
  - Staff results calculation → report → advertiser results.
  - Advertiser billing.
- **Not walked live** (traced in code instead): self-service campaign creation through to funding,
  installation photo upload and review, and a real GPS trip. Uploads hit B1 and a trip needs real
  geolocation. The code findings for them are marked "code".
- **Tests:** no product tests were run, because no product code changed.
- **Already-known items** are referenced, not re-reported. These are the triage report
  `final-inhouse-triage-report.md` (G01, G02, G04, G05), the open requests (REQ-018, REQ-050, REQ-062, …) and
  the leftover demo wording in `docs/demo-data.md:71`. Motorcycles are out of scope because the pilot is
  car-only (REQ-179).

Counts: **21 Broken**, **34 Unclear or unclean**, **11 Decisions for the owner**.

## 1. Broken

Severity: **High** blocks a core journey or shows wrong money/results. **Medium** is a real failure that has a

### B1 · High · Uploads time out before the virus scan runs (applicants, drivers, advertisers)
- **Where:** `/apply?step=details` and `/apply?step=car` (applicant); installation photos (driver); artwork upload (advertiser).
  - The browser waits `frontend/src/lib/files/onboarding-upload.ts:83-105`, `installation-evidence-upload.ts:57`
    and `creative-upload.ts:138`: 40 polls × 1.5 s ≈ 60 s.
  - The scan only runs as a worker cron every `WORKER_SWEEP_INTERVAL_MINUTES` (default 5):
    `app/jobs/worker.py:185-189` and `app/jobs/file_scanning.py:14`. Nothing starts a scan when an upload is confirmed.
- **Repro:** apply as a new driver, add the licence and photo, then press **Send my details**.
- **Actual:**
  - The file shows "Uploading…" for a minute, then the page says "We're still checking your files. Please try
    again in a minute."
  - In the run it failed on the first press of both apply steps (65–75 s each). It went through only on a
    second press after the 5-minute scan had run.
  - Most first attempts will fail unless the cron happens to fire within a minute. Artwork and installation
    photos use the same 60-second limit.
- **Expected:** a file is checked within seconds, or the form accepts it and says "We're checking your file —
  you can carry on".
- **Likely fix:**
  - Enqueue a scan job for the file on upload confirm and keep the cron as a catch-up.
  - Label the wait "Checking file…", not "Uploading…".
  - Optionally let the submit go through while the scan is pending and gate review on a clean scan.
- **Screenshots:** `flows/apply_2_details_sent.png`, `flows/apply_3_car_sent.png`.

### B3 · High · Advertiser results never appear for most campaigns
- **Where:** advertiser `/advertiser/campaigns/{id}/report` and the campaign page.
  - The API returns 409 `DISCLOSURE_SUPPRESSED` or `CAMPAIGN_REPORT_PENDING`.
  - The advertiser copy is in `frontend/src/app/advertiser/campaigns/[campaignId]/report/measurement-authority.tsx:318`
    and `frontend/src/components/ui/data-unavailable.tsx:9`.
- **Repro:** sign in as `advertiser@demo…` and open the results for each campaign.
- **Actual:**
  - Only "Abuja Lunch Routes" shows results.
  - **Suppressed (409):**
    - The completed "Central Area Lunch Deliveries" returns 409, although it has an issued report.
    - The live "Wuse Lunch Rush" (one driver) returns 409.
  - **Not ready:** Utako returns "report pending".
  - **What the advertiser sees:** "Campaign results can't be shown. No results are shown for this campaign at
    the moment", and on the campaign page "Results appear after more trips are recorded". More trips will not
    help a campaign with one car.
  - **Staff side:** staff issuing the report get no warning that the advertiser will see nothing.
  - **Cause:** the privacy floor (at least 3 vehicles, 5 trips and 2 days per area) hides any campaign with
    fewer than 3 cars. The pilot plan of 10 cars across 5 advertisers means 2 cars per advertiser, so most
    pilot advertisers would never see a result.
- **Expected:** the advertiser sees results, or an honest reason and what will change it. Staff are warned before they issue.
- **Likely fix:**
  - Product decision D1.
  - Whatever is decided, show the real reason ("Your campaign has fewer than 3 cars, so area results are
    hidden to protect drivers' privacy") and warn staff on the run screen.
- **Screenshots:** `flows/results_5_advertiser_report.png`, `adv/adv2__advertiser_campaigns_aa50bca6_…_report.png`.

### B4 · High · "Mark all read" fails with "This command does not accept a request body" (the owner's example)
- **Where:** notifications panel, every role.
  - The check is `frontend/src/lib/api/mutation-boundary.ts:51-57`. It rejects a body-less POST if it carries
    *any* `content-type`, *any* `transfer-encoding`, or a `content-length` other than 0.
  - The same rule guards `/api/notifications/{id}/read`, `/api/(advertiser|driver)/files/uploads/{id}/confirm`
    and `/api/admin/files/{id}/installation-review`.
- **Repro:**
  - On a direct local connection the button works (`flows/notify_2_after_mark_all.png`).
  - Sending the same empty POST as a tunnel or edge proxy re-frames it, with `transfer-encoding: chunked` or with
    `content-type: application/json` and `content-length: 0`, returns
    **415 BFF_MEDIA_TYPE_DENIED "This command does not accept a request body"**.
  - Both variants were reproduced. The client preview runs through a Cloudflare quick tunnel (REQ-115), which
    re-frames requests like this.
- **Expected:** an empty request is accepted however it is framed.
- **Likely fix:**
  - Decide by the actual body bytes only (lines 60–72 already read them).
  - Allow a zero-byte chunked body and ignore `content-type` when there is no body.
  - Never show this text to users.
  - This also protects upload confirm and installation review, which would fail the same way behind the tunnel.

## 3. Speed ("the platform is slow while navigating")

### Measured on the local production build (no tunnel)

**Advertiser:**

| Measure | Time |
|---|---|
| Full page load | 0.7–1.2 s |
| In-app navigation | 0.1–0.7 s |
| Retargeting | 2.3 s, with 15 API calls (one per linked source plus one per campaign zone set) |

**Driver:**

| Page | API calls per load |
|---|---|
| Home | 19, including 7 separate "assignments with status X" calls (`frontend/src/lib/driver/load-campaign-journey.ts:195-201`) |
| Track | 17 |

**Staff:**

| Page | API calls |
|---|---|
| Every staff page (badges in `frontend/src/app/admin/layout.tsx:15-22`) | about 18–20, before the page's own reads |
| Advertisers | 74 |
| Campaigns | 42 |
| Campaign hub | 33 |

- Warm staff loads take 1.3–2.1 s.
- Under memory pressure on the shared Docker VM, two loads took 18 s and 27 s.
- The API itself is fast: 7–171 ms per call. The slowness is the number of calls in a row and the host.

**Every navigation:**
- The shell re-reads `/me` and the unread count.
- The notification bell also polls every 45 s (`components/notifications/notification-center.tsx:152`).

**Bundle:**
- Sentry ships in the main chunk even with no DSN: 416 KB raw, 131 KB gzipped (`1cv090751dyjr.js`).
- The map library chunk is 947 KB raw, 246 KB gzipped. It is loaded only on map pages.
- Responses are compressed.

### Top causes, in order

1. **The client's host.** Port 3100 had nothing listening during the audit.
   - The client preview that matches the request history is the Cloudflare quick tunnel at
     `https://sam-sheriff-anybody-engines.trycloudflare.com` (REQ-115). It routes to this laptop, to the
     `cv-req143-main` container (published on 127.0.0.1:18080 and 33439).
   - That container is labelled "Local feasibility harness; not a qualified cloud release image".
   - It has a 2 GiB memory cap, a 192 MB Node heap and **one** API worker. It shares a 3.8 GB Docker VM with
     Supabase and other containers, and every request crosses the internet twice.
   - This alone explains most of the slowness the client felt. A real host (REQ-050 Hetzner) will be much
     faster. Nothing on port 3100 was touched.
2. **Staff layout badges:** about 18 reads on every staff page (`admin/layout.tsx:15-22`). One summary
   endpoint returning all counts would remove 17 round trips per click.
3. **Driver Home makes 7 status reads where one would do** (`load-campaign-journey.ts:195-201`). Use one
   `status=in(...)` list call, or a "current job" endpoint.
4. **Fan-out lists.**
   - Staff Advertisers makes 74 calls, one or more per company. The list endpoint should return the counts it needs.
   - Retargeting makes one call per link and per campaign.
5. **One page, many reads.** The campaign hub makes 33 calls; batch them or load sections on demand (also fixes B11).
6. **Sentry in the main bundle with no DSN.** Load it lazily, or exclude it when no DSN is set.

## 6. Decisions for the owner

Plain-English questions. Each has options and a recommendation.

1. **D1 — Small campaigns can't see results.** Results are hidden unless at least 3 cars drove in the same area. Most pilot advertisers will have 1–2 cars.
   - (a) Lower the rule for a company's own campaign data (for example show totals with no area breakdown).
   - (b) Keep the rule and tell advertisers plainly why.
   - (c) Require 3+ cars per campaign.
   - *Recommend (a):* totals (miles, trips, days, estimated views) for every campaign, with area detail only when 3+ cars.
2. **D2 — Who creates results?** Today staff type a date range in UTC code format to "prepare a results calculation", then issue a report.
   - (a) Make it automatic: weekly and at campaign end.
   - (b) Keep it manual with simple date pickers.
   - (c) Both.
   - *Recommend (c):* automatic weekly and final reports; a simple manual button for re-runs.
3. **D3 — Budget alerts.** The client gave 80 / 95 / 100 %, but the staff panel asks to "record blocked policy state" and the advertiser sees "Not set up yet".
   - (a) Switch on 80/95/100 for every campaign and remove the panel.
   - (b) Let each advertiser choose.
   - *Recommend (a).*
4. **D4 — Quote requests from the website.** They are only emailed to a Gmail address and nothing is kept.
   - (a) Keep them in a "New enquiries" list for staff, with a button to create the advertiser.
   - (b) Email only, as now.
   - *Recommend (a).*
5. **D5 — What advertisers see as money.** The home page shows "Driver pay to date".
   - (a) Show "Spent so far of budget".
   - (b) Show both.
   - *Recommend (a);* driver pay is Terrax's business.
6. **D6 — Retargeting in the main menu.** It is an advanced feature for a first-time user.
   - (a) Move it inside each campaign.
   - (b) Keep it in the menu.
   - *Recommend (a).*
7. **D7 — Self-service cancel.** Advertisers can permanently cancel a running campaign from an always-open panel.
   - (a) Keep it, behind a "Cancel campaign" link and confirm.
   - (b) Make it "Ask Terrax to cancel" (a staff action).
   - *Recommend (b)* for the pilot.
8. **D8 — How applicants continue their application.** Today it is an 80-character code, pasted into each step, valid 30 minutes.
   - (a) An email link that opens the right step.
   - (b) A 6-digit code.
   - (c) As now.
   - *Recommend (a),* with a 24-hour expiry.
9. **D9 — Checking a new driver's bank account when the automatic name check is unavailable.**
   - (a) Staff confirm by a small test transfer and type its reference.
   - (b) Applicants wait until the automatic check works.
   - (c) Staff tick "I checked this" with a note.
   - *Recommend (a),* with any reference length the bank uses.
10. **D10 — Colours and the theme picker.** The client wants brand colours with more white, and dislikes the current colour.
    - (a) One theme from the brand guide (white background, brand dark-green text, green→red only in the logo and highlights) and remove the picker.
    - (b) Keep the picker for staff only.
    - (c) Keep it for everyone.
    - *Recommend (a):* one brand theme for advertiser and driver, built from Direction 5 and the brand guide, with the Terrax logo replacing the "Cardvert." wordmark.
11. **D11 — Driver map of where the job pays.** Drivers aren't shown where their miles count.
    - (a) Show the campaign area on the driver's Home and Jobs (needs MapTiler, REQ-050).
    - (b) Show a list of area names.
    - *Recommend (a),* with (b) until the map account exists.
