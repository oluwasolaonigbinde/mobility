# 2. Verdict

## **PAUSE DEPENDENT SLICES AND REVISE**

The architecture does **not** need a fundamental technology redesign. The modular monolith, Postgres/PostGIS source of truth, one recoverable worker, Next.js BFF, contract baselines, provider adapters, and formula-first MVP are appropriate for one city, 15–50 vehicles, 2–3 advertisers, and the stated ~10× path.

It is nevertheless unsafe to continue the current sequence as written. The blockers sit at domain seams, so implementing the next dependent slice would harden contradictory behavior:

- Pause **S3 release/disbursement** until F02–F05 are resolved and adversarially tested.
- Pause **paid campaign activation/cancellation** until F06–F08 are specified transactionally.
- Pause **real-driver GPS collection** until F01, F09–F11, F16–F17 pass; continue a synthetic-data/native feasibility spike.
- Pause **advertiser heatmaps and all live Module G ingestion/display/export** until F09–F15 pass at the relevant gate.
- Continue safe work: architecture amendments, contract/test harnesses, provider-neutral staging with synthetic data, native spike, file/billing scaffolding that does not activate live processing, and implemented-foundation hardening.

The three highest-leverage corrections are: **(1)** one explicit trip/campaign/money finality model with transactional guards; **(2)** one advertiser-release contract combining measurement methodology, proof, privacy, and disclosure control; and **(3)** an immediate native/finality and synthetic-staging proof lane, so W4 is hardening rather than discovery.

This verdict assesses the documented architecture only. Implementation conformance remains unverified because the code was out of scope; measurement accuracy remains uncertain until Lagos calibration/holdout data exists; mobile feasibility and battery behavior require real devices; bank correctness requires reconciliation tests; security requires technical testing; and lawful bases, DPIA residual risk, DCPMI status, retention periods, and release shape require qualified Nigerian privacy advice. Those uncertainties are gates, not silent assumptions.

# 3. Per-dimension commentary

## 3.1 Requirements coverage

Every Module A–G and §9 outcome has at least a named architectural home, which is better than a missing-module design. Mechanical placement is not the same as implementation-ready coverage. Module C’s binding background/offline native behavior is a two-item sketch; Module E’s release-to-cash path contains contradictions; Module F’s existing heatmap violates its own future privacy rule; Module G has tables but not a safe or truthful end-to-end workflow. Month 3’s native deliverable is deferred to W4, while Month 5 carries first deployment, store risk, stabilization, exports, training, and handover.

The proposed retargeting scope can remain commercially useful without direct ad-platform integration: typed aggregate planning sources → campaign/zone linkage → privacy-controlled coverage/modelled-contact aggregates → contextual follow-up recommendations. It is only partially covered because the current free-form source, consent, query, methodology, and report contracts do not make that chain safe or reproducible. The detailed classifications are in §4.

## 3.2 Architectural soundness

Thin routes, service-owned business logic, Postgres/PostGIS truth, disposable Redis, DB-derived worker sweeps, a BFF boundary, contract baselines, ports/adapters, partitioning, and append-only audit are sound for this pilot and ~10× path. No microservices, Kafka, Kubernetes, WebSockets, multi-region, or separate analytics store are justified.

The structural defect is mistaking local idempotency for end-to-end finality. A deduplicated batch does not prove trip completeness; an idempotent job cannot cure a prematurely frozen money fact; a service pre-check does not serialize concurrent vehicle assignments. The corrected design needs three explicit transaction boundaries: client-finalized/server-sealed trip, activation/cancellation snapshot, and reconciled cash batch. For each, document state/guard table, idempotency identity, database lock/constraint, repair path, and invariant test. At ~10×, scale app/worker replicas and managed Postgres first; split analytics only on measured contention or independent deploy/team need.

## 3.3 Domain benchmark

Formula-based estimation is the correct MVP choice. The missing element is methodology governance, not a more complex algorithm. Recognized OOH practice distinguishes gross opportunity, opportunity/likelihood to see, audience and reach; uses inventory/visibility and movement/traffic evidence; and requires transparent source, model, proof, limitation and correction disclosures. [S4]–[S6]

The product should separate:

- **Measured operational facts:** approved installation interval, route/time/distance, GPS quality, campaign/vehicle state.
- **Modelled potential contacts:** formula-derived opportunity estimate with input-quality grade and uncertainty.
- **Not measured by this MVP:** verified human view, unique reach, demographics, online-person match, conversion, or causal attribution.

Every issued report should point to one immutable measurement run and proof-of-performance manifest; calibration should report bias/error/coverage and a holdout; corrections should reissue rather than overwrite. There is no present requirement to design camera hardware, inference, training, or video storage. The service isolation and formula versions avoid a wholesale dead end, but the MVP amendment should define a versioned `measurement_observation` input with source kind, time/space scope, provenance, quality, and consent/classification so future sensor/count observations can join a new formula version without replacing campaign, GPS, privacy, payout, or reporting domains.

## 3.4 Privacy and compliance

P7 correctly recognizes raw GPS as personal data, but the target delegates too much to “consent wording.” The legal control plane must be architecture: controller/processor roles, DPIA, processing inventory, purpose and lawful basis, consent/objection/withdrawal where applicable, recipients/transfers, retention, DSR, breach, and evidence. [S1][S2]

At this scale, that does not require a privacy microservice. Versioned policy/configuration records, one disclosure-control service, a ticketed manual DSR runbook, managed encryption/KMS, audited privileged reads, and a subprocessor register are proportionate. A vehicle is not the data subject, k alone does not anonymize sparse location, and gating export alone does not gate Module G processing. The company should document whether DCPMI/DPO/DPCO duties apply to its actual corporate/service facts and reassess on material scope/scale change; applicability remains a **HYPOTHESIS** pending those facts and qualified advice.

## 3.5 Money correctness

The hourly classifier, integer seconds, cap-before-price, one 2dp quantization, version-preserving v1/v2 split, advisory locking, write-once calculation, and append-only differentials are strong. The failures sit downstream: the wrong calendar-day allocation, review state disagreeing with release, report netting without cash-debt allocation, and an underdefined manual batch.

The architecture should publish these equations with exact status/allocation semantics:

\[
\text{earned\_net}=\sum \text{credits}-\sum \text{reversals}
\]

\[
\text{carry\_forward\_debt}=\max(0,\text{unallocated post-payment reversals})
\]

\[
\text{batch\_payable}=\max(0,\text{available credits}-\text{allocated debt})
\]

`cash_paid` must derive only from reconciled batch lines. Test cross-midnight intervals, two workers, review/release races, corrections before/after payment, partial bank outcomes, duplicate export, void/retry, and payee-data change after approval. Manual bank transfer is appropriate for the pilot; missing reconciliation is not.

## 3.6 Mobile reality check

The explicit Start/End boundary and batch envelope are good foundations but do not satisfy D11’s background/offline promise. Android and iOS can throttle, terminate, or deny tracking, so background GPS must be an observable best-effort state with measurable completeness, never an unconditional guarantee. [S7]–[S13]

The smallest robust client is an encrypted append queue; stable point/batch identities; ACK-then-delete; finalization watermark; server seal; visible `active/degraded/stopped/syncing`; last-fix/upload age; and ops/dispute path. Android and iOS share the server contract, not necessarily runtime logic. A 1 Hz row estimate is not an SLO: derive adaptive sampling/batching from the payout/geofence error budget and verify four-hour battery drain, accuracy, gap rate, and sync latency on low/mid devices. Native refresh must be per-device, rotating, reuse-detecting, revocable, and secure-storage backed.

## 3.7 Delivery risk

The sequence postpones native tracking and deployment—the largest empirical risks—until the final wave. During W2, run three lanes: commercial lifecycle work only after its state-table amendment; a deliberately thin native GPS/finality spike; and synthetic-data staging plus privacy/measurement/ops evidence. W3 consumes those proven contracts. W4 integrates, hardens, trains and pilots.

The likely schedule failures are store/background permission approval, late trip-contract redesign, undefined commercial states, Q31 evidence/legal review, and first production-like failures arriving with no stabilization runway. Simplify native UI to the promised driver flows; keep conditional ad-platform integrations, automated disbursement, AI counting, and elaborate DSR automation out. The delivery status accurately says nothing is deployed and W2–W4 are unstarted, so pilot readiness is not yet verifiable.

## 3.8 Unknown unknowns

The document set lacks several artifacts a principal architect would expect before exposing real money/location data: a single cross-domain transition/locking table; a data dictionary for commercial and measurement facts; an administrator permission/maker-checker matrix; a privacy data-flow/processing inventory; an advertiser claim catalogue and issued-report schema; an incident/breach/on-call ownership matrix; a vendor/region register; and explicit acceptance/SLO budgets for location, worker lag, report latency, money reconciliation, and recovery.

These are not requests for heavyweight governance. Each can be a short versioned table/runbook. Their absence matters because future coding agents otherwise invent incompatible answers slice by slice. Implementation conformity, financial reconciliation, security behavior, mobile reliability, measurement validity, and qualified legal approval all remain outside the packet’s evidence boundary.

# 6. Unusually good decisions

1. **Formula-first MVP with AI explicitly post-MVP.** That is the right evidence/complexity tradeoff; the correction is methodology and calibration, not premature computer vision.
2. **Pilot-sized modular monolith plus ports/adapters.** One service boundary per domain and conditional direct-ad integrations preserve options without microservice overhead.
3. **Postgres as durable truth with disposable Redis and DB-derived sweeps.** This makes queue loss recoverable and keeps failure semantics understandable.
4. **Append-only, versioned money with explicit rounding and correction entries.** D9’s v1/v2 split, single quantization, input fingerprints, and no silent history rewrite are excellent foundations.
5. **Contract/BFF discipline and lifecycle evidence.** Synchronized OpenAPI baselines, browser/FastAPI separation, partition coverage alarms, atomic purge evidence, and acknowledged backup-retention behavior are unusually concrete.

# 7. Five questions that must be answered before trusting the architecture

1. **What exact client/server event proves that a trip is complete, all intended offline batches are received or intentionally excluded, and the server may seal it for money?**
2. **What does one naira of campaign “spend” mean contractually, and which immutable payment fact authorizes production versus activation?**
3. **What bank artifact, batch state, and named approver/reconciler make a payout final, including partial failures and later driver debt?**
4. **Who is controller/processor for each GPS, KYC, advertiser, and Module G purpose, and where is the approved DPIA/lawful-basis/retention/DSR evidence for the exact pilot release shape?**
5. **Where is the advertiser-approved impression/exposure methodology, its proof-of-performance binding, and its Lagos calibration/holdout evidence?**
